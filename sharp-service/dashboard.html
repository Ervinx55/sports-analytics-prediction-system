GOOD PASS = a real near-candidate failed a gate and then lost. MISSED WIN = a real near-candidate failed a gate but would have won. HARD PASS = it was never close enough to qualify; its outcome is still tracked, but it is not counted as a missed opportunity. Equivalent Hits 0.5 / Total Bases 0.5 props are counted once. Once a tracked game starts, the system checks official MLB status every 5 minutes until Final and grades every tracked decision.
